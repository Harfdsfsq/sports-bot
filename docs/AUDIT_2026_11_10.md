1. A-only final live selection controlled by DAILY_LIVE_A_ONLY=true. B retains
   its classification and prospective one-total-per-fixture trial. No probability,
   EV, sample or historical gates were relaxed, no challenger promoted.
2. Discovery merge uses the existing squad-aware identity contract: both teams,
   original orientation, <=30-minute kickoff difference. Invalid candidates are
   excluded before ranking, rather than blocking a valid second-best identity.
   Prevent first-team/U21/reserve conflation and six-hour cross-fixture merges.

   quote, form or sporting context was actually received. Admitted runtime
   registry remains the authority. This can lower displayed coverage honestly.

   repeated page, shared quota denial/error or bounded request cap. Exposes
   pagination_complete and pagination_stop; detects servers ignoring offsets.

   credential detection and cache behavior are preserved. No unlimited loops.
6. Runtime actionable-window SLA: ready percentage, 90/95 status and number of
   additional paired matches needed for 95%; empty window is not 100% coverage.

## Intended operating contract and rollout

Maintain up to 300 real daily fixtures from the union of feeds. Distinguish
available inventory, future actionable fixtures, supported/priced fixtures and
actually enriched fixtures; do not make 95% by shrinking denominators secretly.
Aim for >=90%, ideally >=95%, actual fresh line+admitted-context coverage in the
30-minute–4-hour window. Some days fewer than 300 supported matches exist.
Unsupported/empty/error/throttled/unattempted gaps must stay distinguishable.

A requires the existing q>=78, confidence>=70, EV>=5%, edge>=3pp, supported
samples and agreement, exact fresh price and Telegram acknowledgement. These
scores do NOT mean 75–80% win probability. B stays paper-only. Do not impose a
75% model-probability gate on an uncalibrated model and advertise it as accuracy.
Do not search only high odds: at price 1.5 break-even is 66.7%; at 2.0 it is 50%.
The objective is accurate probabilities and positive executable EV with uncertainty.
