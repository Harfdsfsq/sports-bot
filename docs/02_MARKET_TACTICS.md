# Market tactics

## Main-channel markets
### 1) Totals (primary)
Best market for stable daily output when line and xG structure agree.

Publish only when:
- line is between **2.0 and 3.25**
- 2+ bookmakers confirm the angle
- no heavy-shrink single-source profile
- under bets have enough negative xG headroom
- over bets have enough positive xG headroom

Prefer:
- under when expected total is clearly below the line
- over when expected total is clearly above the line

Avoid:
- under in wild matches with total xG above ~4.6
- over in slow matches with total xG below ~2.0
- cup totals with only one real source

### 2) Draw-no-bet / AH0 (primary)
Best side market when a team edge exists but variance is still meaningful.

Publish only when:
- odds roughly **1.80 to 3.20**
- xG side difference is material
- 2+ bookmakers confirm
- no cup + non-core + heavy-shrink stack

### 3) H2H (secondary)
Use only when team edge is strong and market is not too wide.

Publish only when:
- odds roughly **1.80 to 2.90** in core profile
- 2+ bookmakers confirm
- not single-source heavy-shrink
- not cup underdog shots in the 3.0+ range

## Shadow-only markets
- BTTS unless enough positive history is built
- team totals without strong provider coverage
- non-core cup sides with odds above 3.20
- anything with `single-source + heavy-shrink`

## Hard no-publish combinations
- `single-source + non-core + heavy-shrink`
- `cup + odds >= 3.20 + only 2 books`
- `xG side diff < 0.25` on a high-odds side bet
- `odds >= 3.50` with weak or mixed form signals

## Daily operating rule
If the run has only risky candidates, publish nothing or keep them in shadow.
A clean empty run is better than a weak “best bet”.
