There were 30 near-window fixtures: 14 with fresh lines, 21 with sporting

about later coverage.

What remains unknown: whether other bookmakers have quotes for these events,
or whether the vendor has a scheduled event with no current market anywhere.
No unsupported assertion that DraftKings/FanDuel will cover them was made.

## Implemented repairs

1. Record only actual quote-request targets (or completed discovery misses).
   Fixtures omitted by the request cap or incomplete discovery no longer acquire
   an invented empty-result cooldown. They stay eligible for the next run.
2. Keep per-event, per-account outcomes: not queried, absent event in response,
   empty bookmaker data, no parseable supported full-match market, failed request,
   or rejected fixture identity/status. A malformed HTTP-200 response is an
   error, not evidence of absent prices.
3. Before parsing daily quotes, verify event ID, pending status, both teams in
   home/away order and kickoff within 15 minutes. An event ID alone cannot turn
   a live, reversed or changed fixture response into a prematch quote.
4. For successful empty selected-book snapshots, optionally inspect
   `/events/{id}`: at most 4 extra requests per run and 16 per UTC day, under
   the existing shared/account quotas. Cache metadata receipts for 30 minutes
   and revalidate their fixture binding on reuse. Failures count toward the
   diagnostic budget. Auth, rate-limit and service failures stop further probes.
   Metadata never creates an offer or changes bookmaker selections.
5. Persist the safe diagnostic under the registry attempt and expose it in
   `latest-actionable-coverage-gaps.json` and source previews. No API key or
   authenticated URL is persisted by this diagnostic.
6. Include this input transformation in the forward fingerprint and declare V6.
   Earlier declarations/results remain in history; no past experiment is credited
   as a forward result. The live publication gate remains closed.
