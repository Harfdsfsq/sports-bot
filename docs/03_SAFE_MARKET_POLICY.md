# Safe market policy

Use this publication bias until the data quality improves:

## Primary publish bias
- core totals
- safer side markets like DNB / AH0
- moderate odds only

## Avoid as main publish
- high-odds away sides
- single-source + heavy-shrink combinations
- non-core / cup profiles unless they survive audit cleanly

## Practical target range
- odds roughly `1.70 - 2.80`
- at least 2 books
- no suspicious odds integrity report
