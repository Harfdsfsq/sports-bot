# Bankroll and staking

## Objective
Protect the bankroll first. Grow only from positive expectation, not from volume.

## Main rule
No martingale. No step-up after losses. No chase.

## Tiering
- **A-tier**: strongest publishable candidate -> 2.0% to 2.5% stake
- **B-tier**: acceptable but not elite -> 1.0% to 1.5% stake
- **C-tier**: shadow only -> 0%

## Portfolio caps
- max open exposure: 10% to 11%
- max single stake in production: 3.0% to 3.25%
- kelly fraction: 0.20 to 0.22

## What not to do
- do not raise stake because the last bets lost
- do not raise stake because odds look attractive
- do not treat cups and non-core leagues as equal to core leagues

## Interpretation
A profile that publishes slightly fewer bets but with lower variance is much more useful than a noisy profile that produces daily action but unstable bankroll behavior.
